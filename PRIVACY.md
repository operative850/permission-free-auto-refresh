# Privacy Policy

Permission-free Auto Refresh does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

**What the extension stores**
Your chosen theme (dark or light) and your last used refresh interval. These are saved in your own browser with `localStorage` and never leave your computer.

**What the extension does not do**
- It does not read page content, URLs, titles, history or cookies.
- It does not make network requests.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not share anything with third parties.

**Permissions**
The extension requests no permissions.

**Contact**
Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
